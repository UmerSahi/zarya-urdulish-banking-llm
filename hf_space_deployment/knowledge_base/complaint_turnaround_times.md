# Zarya Digital Bank — Regulatory Complaint Turnaround Times (TAT)
*Mandated by State Bank of Pakistan Consumer Protection Regulations*

## 1. Fund Transfers & Interbank Disputes
- **Raast P2P Dispute Settlement:** 24 hours (Automated reconciliation cycle).
- **1Link Interbank Fund Transfer (IBFT) Deduction without Credit:** 24 to 48 working hours.
- **Erroneous / Wrong Account Transfer Recall Notice:** 72 hours (3 to 5 working days for beneficiary bank consent reversal).

## 2. ATM Machine Disputes
- **Zarya Bank ATM Cash Retraction (Same Bank):** 48 working hours.
- **1Link Other-Bank ATM Cash Retraction Dispute:** 7 to 10 working days (requires interbank switch settlement).
- **ATM Swallowed / Captured Card:** Destruction protocol enforced; replacement card dispatched in 3 business days.

## 3. Cards & Account Logistics
- **New / Replacement Debit Card Delivery:** 3 to 5 business days via courier across Pakistan.
- **Emergency Lost / Stolen Card Block:** Instantaneous (real-time in-app lock within 60 seconds).
- **Senior Grievance Escalation (P1 Priority):** First response within 4 working hours.
