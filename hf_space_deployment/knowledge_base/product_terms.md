# Zarya Digital Bank — Product Terms & Account Features

## 1. Zarya PLS Digital Savings Account
- **Current Indicative Profit Rate:** 17.50% per annum (subject to SBP Monetary Policy rate).
- **Calculation Basis:** Daily closing product balance.
- **Profit Distribution:** Credited on the 1st of every calendar month.
- **Withholding Tax on Profit:** 15.0% for FBR Active Tax Filers; 30.0% for Non-Filers.

## 2. Zarya Freelancer Digital Account (Exporters Special FCY)
- **Currency Support:** PKR and USD digital multi-currency wallets.
- **Foreign Earnings Retention:** Freelancers can retain up to 50% of inbound earnings in USD wallet.
- **Monthly Inbound Limit:** Up to USD 10,000 equivalent per month.

## 3. Account Reactivation & Dormancy Terms
- **Dormancy Trigger:** Accounts with zero customer-initiated transactions for 365 consecutive days.
- **Reactivation Procedure:** 100% digital via Zarya App (NADRA live biometric scan + deposit/transfer of PKR 100). Zero branch visit required. Reactivation window: 1 to 2 hours.
