# Zarya Digital Bank — Official Schedule of Charges (SOC)
*Effective Date: Current Fiscal Year (Subject to SBP Circular Updates)*

## 1. Debit Cards Annual Issuance & Maintenance Fees
- **PayPak Classic Debit Card:** PKR 2,400 + Tax per annum (Updated from PKR 1,850).
- **Visa Platinum Debit Card:** PKR 3,800 + Tax per annum.
- **Mastercard Gold Debit Card:** PKR 3,200 + Tax per annum.
- **Virtual Digital Debit Card (In-App Only):** PKR 0 (Completely Free of charge).
- **Card Replacement / Lost Card Fee:** PKR 1,200 for PayPak; PKR 1,800 for Visa/Mastercard.

## 2. ATM Cash Withdrawal & In-Branch Charges
- **Zarya Digital Bank ATMs:** Free cash withdrawals across all digital kiosks.
- **1Link Interbank ATMs (Other Bank ATMs):** PKR 23.44 per cash withdrawal transaction.
- **ATM Balance Inquiry (Other Bank 1Link ATM):** PKR 3.13 per inquiry.
- **Cash Deposit at Zarya Partner Franchises:** PKR 0 (Completely Free, zero agent surcharge).

## 3. Digital Payment & Remittance Taxes
- **Inward Home Remittance (SBP PRI Channel):** Free (Zero charges for recipient).
- **International Online / POS Card Spend Advance Withholding Tax (WHT):**
  * Active Filer on FBR Active Taxpayer List (ATL): 5.0%
  * Non-Filer: 10.0%
- **SMS Transaction Alert Service:** Free digital push notifications; optional physical telecom SMS: PKR 150 + FED monthly.
