# Zarya Digital Bank — Transaction Limits Schedule
*Governed by State Bank of Pakistan Digital Banking Regulations*

## 1. Interbank Fund Transfer (IBFT) & Raast Limits
- **Raast P2P (Person-to-Person) Default Daily Limit:** PKR 200,000 per day.
- **Raast P2P Upgraded Biometric Daily Limit:** PKR 500,000 per day.
- **1Link IBFT Default Daily Limit:** PKR 500,000 per day.
- **1Link IBFT Enhanced Biometric Daily Limit:** Up to PKR 1,000,000 per day (via app facial biometric verification).
- **Per Transaction Cap:** PKR 250,000 per single IBFT transaction.

## 2. Card POS & ATM Withdrawal Limits
- **PayPak Classic:** Daily ATM cash withdrawal PKR 50,000; Daily POS shopping PKR 100,000.
- **Visa Platinum:** Daily ATM cash withdrawal PKR 150,000; Daily POS shopping PKR 500,000.
- **International Online E-Commerce:** Custom in-app limit adjustable from PKR 5,000 up to PKR 300,000 per 24-hour session.

## 3. Asaan Digital Account Tiers
- **Total Monthly Debit Limit:** PKR 1,000,000.
- **Maximum Account Credit Balance at any time:** PKR 1,000,000.
